How to read this dashboard

  • Each dot is one store. Excellent (90%+), Meets standard (80–89%), Development required (below 80%), not audited yet this month.
  • Every store needs one Operations Checklist audit a month. Coverage is how many stores have been audited.
  • The audit has 39 checkpoints across 5 zones: Planning, Customer Experience, Backend, People and Business.
  • Action points lists every checkpoint marked NOT MET, with the auditor's remark and the agreed action. Repeat means the same point also failed last month.
  • Tap a store to see its auditor (AIC), manager (BM), zone scores, remarks, photos, and to download the audit as PDF.
  • Store managers can tap Reply / mark as fixed on any NOT MET point to say what was done and add a photo. It saves to the Ops sheet and shows as Store replied.
